## What this admits

Setup previews by default. Apply requires explicit selection, a fresh
Issue-issued admission and exact byte guards, and creates only approved
directories and manifest binding. It does not stage, publish or close an Issue.
Reading requires independently qualified existing documents and exact selections.
New users without those inputs stop after setup.

SPEC authoring/import, approved iterations, approval, durable iteration-to-Issue
binding and SPEC-guarded repository publication remain unavailable. No Goal
writer, Service/API authority or Experience capability is added.
Currentness checks observed state, not every write; confinement is cooperative,
not continuous. Declared filesystem actor evidence is not authenticated identity.
Do not overlay a6 and SPEC installations or discover a substitute environment
after refusal.

The carried command sequence and bootstrap addendum remain drafts, not frozen
`AGENTS.md` instructions. Publication does not allocate contract 1.3.0 or
authorize an external evaluation. Consumer record mutation still needs its own
approved Issue and supported tooling.
