exact bytes even though all use `sha256:<hex>`. A normalized semantic manifest
digest cannot substitute for a byte-currentness guard. No automatic stale-guard
refresh or authority fallback is admitted.

Issue admission. Exit success requires verified whole-context completion. Late
refusals preserve publication, applied/unknown effects and cleanup/index debt:
never automatically retry known publication or invent rollback. Repository
publication and Issue closeout remain separate; historical unknowns stay unknown.
