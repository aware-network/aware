## Authority and evidence limits

Source selection is not write authority. Real Issue admission, exact owner,
lifecycle, scope and byte-currentness guards remain mandatory. The Issue source,
Protocol `source_sha256` and snapshot-input digest identify different exact bytes,
despite sharing `sha256:<hex>` encoding. A normalized semantic manifest digest
cannot substitute for its source-byte guard. No automatic stale-guard refresh.

Preview is non-authorizing; apply requires explicit intent and fresh admission.
Whole-context completion governs success. Late refusals can retain known
publication, applied/unknown effects or cleanup debt: never automatically retry
known publication, erase evidence or invent rollback. Publication and Issue
closeout remain separate. Historical unknown receipts stay unknown.

No SPEC approval/import, approved-iteration authoring, durable iteration-to-Issue
writer, Goal writer/dispatch, Service/API, ontology/ORM or Experience authority.
Filesystem actor evidence is declared, not authenticated. Currentness checks
observed state; every-write detection and continuous confinement are unsupported.
Conservative component notices do not prove exact binary linkage or confer a
legal warranty. Bounded disclosure scanning is not exhaustive secret detection.
