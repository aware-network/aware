# Consumer notice treatment

Aware-authored files retain the existing Apache-2.0 policy. Each newly projected
Aware wheel contains the complete license and a scoped NOTICE. Consumer source
and the exact source/export dispositions accompany the distribution. The three
Protocol wheels and six schema dependencies are byte-preserved inputs from the
already reviewed read-only bundle; their original notices are retained.

The source-derived `jsonschema 4.26.0+aware.1` excludes the benchmark subtree;
it is not represented as the unmodified registry wheel. Acquisition and build
inputs are in the separate public [source capsule](../source/README.md).
The schema attachment carries the complete upstream license file (both BSD and
AFL texts) with **BSD-3-Clause selected**, alongside the package MIT notice and
the 20-resource/five-adaptation accounting. Those are distinct licensing inputs.

`rpds` retains the previously reviewed conservative crate/Rust-library legal
account. Its precautionary texts are not a claim that every component is linked.

Four additional publisher wheels supply the existing owner's Pydantic contract
dependency: Pydantic, pydantic-core, annotated-types and typing-inspection. Each
exact wheel hash is checked against its PyPI publication; the complete packaged
legal files are copied into `notices/packages` without modification.

For the `pydantic-core` binary, `notices/pydantic-core` includes its publisher
CycloneDX SBOM plus **103 crate candidates and 185 legal files**. Crate archives
match the SHA-256 recorded in that exact wheel's SBOM. Where `wit-bindgen-rt`
omits legal files from its crate archive, the three complete root legal texts
are acquired at the VCS revision recorded in that archive. The `r-efi` AUTHORS
file carries its permission, disclaimers and attribution; it is retained whole.

For permissive OR alternatives we select MIT where offered, otherwise Apache-2.0
where offered (retaining LLVM exceptions where required), and otherwise retain
the stated permissive terms such as BSD, Zlib or Unicode. Required AND terms,
including Unicode, remain applicable. All collected alternatives are preserved
without relabeling upstream code as Aware Apache; `r-efi` uses its MIT option,
not the LGPL option. The manifest retains each original expression and text hash.

This is conservative distribution notice treatment under the delegated OSS
preparation decision, not exact binary linkage, publisher-toolchain identity,
reproducible-build proof, exhaustive authorship certification or legal advice.
The existing Rust-library attachment is version-qualified precautionary input,
not a claim that it identifies pydantic-core's compiler. Publisher references
and attributions are deliberately retained, including harmless upstream CI paths.

Source, wheel resources, legal texts and public instructions are included in
the digest-bound archive. No credentials, private client records or services are
intended for this delivery. Report concrete notice or provenance findings through
GitHub Issues; future payload changes require fresh archive-bound verification.
