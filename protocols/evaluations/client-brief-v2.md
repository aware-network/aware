# Agent-first evaluation brief v2

Use only the approved customer target, installed Aware command and public consumer
instructions to achieve the supplied outcome. Do not seek the development monorepo,
internal proof drivers, private state, hidden answer keys or alternate installations.

Begin by discovering supported capabilities. An empty/new target and an existing
repository are different starting conditions. Do not manufacture a seed commit,
Goal, author, ownership or scope to bypass a refusal. Preparation does not admit
implementation; publication and remote push require their separate permissions.
Use the installed tools for Issue authority and scoped publication.

Record actual reads, operations, confusion, questions, errors and assistance.
Report a blocker as soon as it occurs; unrun work is not failure evidence or a pass.
Separate documented support from the broader outcome you wanted. Reading public
implementation/tests or receiving Human hints must be disclosed, not hidden as
unassisted discovery. Do not run dangerous negative probes on valuable data.

Guided verification comes after the unassisted segment is frozen. Recovery uses a
new execution without earlier transcripts. Durable records should carry the work.
Evidence reports can be authored directly; customer authority fields cannot.
Sanitize before sharing. Evaluations never accept a Goal or authorize a release.
